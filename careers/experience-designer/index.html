Experience Designer

Research, design and test digital experiences that make complex services feel simple.

Design · Hybrid · Full-time

About the role

You'll lead design on client teams — from discovery research to high-fidelity prototypes — making sure what we build works for the people who use it, including those with access needs.

What you’ll do

  • Plan and run user research and turn insights into clear design direction
  • Design journeys, prototypes and interfaces, and test them with real users
  • Champion accessibility and inclusive design across the team
  • Work hand in hand with engineers through delivery

What you’ll bring

  • A portfolio showing end-to-end service or product design work
  • Experience running research and usability testing
  • Strong working knowledge of accessibility standards
  • Comfortable presenting and facilitating with clients

What we offer

  • Flexible working. Hybrid by default, with the trust to shape your week around your work and your life.
  • Learning budget. An annual budget and protected time for courses, certifications and conferences.
  • Health & wellbeing. Comprehensive health cover, mental-health support and wellbeing days.
  • Time to recharge. Generous paid leave, plus your birthday off and company-wide recharge days.
  • Meaningful work. Projects that matter, with clients who want to build something real together.
  • Grow your way. Clear career paths for specialists and leaders, with mentors who invest in you.

We welcome applicants of every background and are happy to discuss adjustments to the process — just let us know.
