Cloud & platforms

We design, migrate, and run cloud platforms that are secure, cost aware, and ready for the data and AI work ahead.

The cloud is no longer a destination. It is the foundation for nearly everything your teams want to do next, from faster releases to data and AI at scale. We help you plan the right path, move workloads with care, and build internal platforms that make it easy for developers to do the right thing. Along the way, we put security, reliability, and cost management in place from the start, so your cloud environment stays healthy and affordable as it grows instead of becoming another source of surprises.

Our cloud & platforms expertise

Cloud strategy and migration

We assess your applications, choose the right approach for each one, and plan migrations in sensible waves that reduce risk, keep the business running, and show value early in the journey.

Platform engineering

We build internal developer platforms with paved paths for building, testing, and deploying software, so teams spend less time on setup and more time shipping features customers notice.

Cloud cost management

We give finance and engineering a shared view of cloud spending, find waste, set budgets and alerts, and build habits that keep costs in line with the value each workload delivers.

Security and resilience

We design secure landing zones, identity controls, and recovery plans that protect your data and keep critical systems available, with automated checks that catch problems before they reach production.

Questions, answered

Should we move everything to the cloud?

Not necessarily. Some systems benefit greatly from moving, others are better modernized first, and a few should stay where they are or be retired. We assess each workload on its merits and recommend the approach that makes the most sense for it.

How do you keep cloud costs from growing out of control?

We set up tagging, budgets, and dashboards so every team can see what it spends. Then we right-size resources, use commitments wisely, and build regular reviews into the rhythm of the business so savings last beyond the first cleanup effort.

Do you work with more than one cloud provider?

Yes. We are independent and work across the major cloud providers. We help you choose based on your needs, skills, and existing investments, and we design for portability where it matters without adding complexity you do not need.
