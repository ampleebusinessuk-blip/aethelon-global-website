Managed IT

Give people one dependable place to get help

We look after everyday support and the maintenance work that prevents everyday problems from becoming outages.

Typical coverage

  • User support and request management
  • Device, server, network, and cloud monitoring
  • Microsoft 365 administration
  • Patching, backup checks, and maintenance
  • Joiners, movers, leavers, and access changes
  • Vendor coordination and regular service reporting

Clear responsibility matters

A managed service works when everyone knows what is covered, how to ask for help, what counts as urgent, and who owns recurring problems. We document those rules before the service begins.

Baseline

Inventory systems, accounts, contracts, risks, recurring issues, and documentation.

Stabilize

Fix urgent support, backup, security, monitoring, and access gaps.

Operate

Handle requests, maintain systems, communicate incidents, and track trends.

Improve

Use service data to remove recurring problems and plan sensible upgrades.

Support feels inconsistent?

We can review the current service and show where ownership or coverage is unclear.
