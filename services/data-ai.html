Data & AI

Get the data right before adding intelligence

Useful AI starts with reliable information, a clear task, and an owner who can judge the result.

Where we help

  • Data quality and ownership
  • Reporting and executive dashboards
  • System integration and shared definitions
  • Workflow and document automation
  • AI use-case assessment and pilots
  • Access, privacy, review, and governance

Start small enough to learn

We prefer a narrow, measurable first use case over a broad AI program with no clear owner. The pilot should show whether the data, workflow, controls, and economics make sense.

Choose the decision or task

Define who needs help, what they do today, and what a better result means.

Check the information

Confirm data quality, permissions, sensitivity, and availability.

Run a controlled pilot

Test with real users, review errors, and keep human judgment where it matters.

Decide whether to scale

Compare the value with cost, risk, support, and change required.

Have an AI idea but not a plan?

We can help you test whether it is useful, feasible, and safe enough to pursue.
