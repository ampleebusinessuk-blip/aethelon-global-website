IT consulting

A technology plan people can follow

We help leadership teams decide what to fix, what to replace, what to leave alone, and how to pay for it.

Useful when

  • The technology budget keeps rising without a clear plan
  • Different teams are buying overlapping tools
  • A major project needs an experienced owner
  • You need an independent view before choosing a vendor
  • Security or continuity risks are not clearly understood
  • The business has outgrown informal IT decisions

What you receive

The output is not a shelf document. It is a prioritized plan with costs, owners, dependencies, and decisions that leadership can review and teams can use.

Current state

Systems, contracts, risks, pain points, spend, and responsibilities.

Priorities

Roadmap

Sequence work around budget, capacity, and business deadlines.

Delivery support

Help with vendors, governance, decisions, and project recovery.
