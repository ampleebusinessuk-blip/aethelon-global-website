## The challenge

The franchise had no shortage of attention. Its games filled the stadium, and its social channels reached millions of people. But the club knew very little about most of those fans. Ticketing, merchandise, the mobile app and the loyalty program all ran on separate systems, so a season ticket holder and a first-time visitor often received the same generic messages.

The app was a particular pain point. It was slow on matchdays, hard to navigate and mostly used to show tickets at the gate. The loyalty program had complicated rules, and many members did not know how to earn or use their points. Leaders wanted to turn broad awareness into lasting relationships, especially with younger fans and families who might not attend every game.

## Our approach

We spent time with fans before designing anything, from interviews in the concourse to watching how people used the app on game day. Those insights shaped a plan focused on a few changes fans would notice right away.

- **A unified fan profile.** We connected ticketing, retail and app data so the club could recognize fans across every touchpoint, with clear consent choices for each person.
- **A rebuilt mobile app.** Our [product engineering](/services/product-engineering/) team rebuilt the app for speed under heavy matchday traffic, with mobile ordering, seat upgrades and simpler navigation.
- **A loyalty program fans understand.** We cut the rules down to a few clear ways to earn and redeem, including experiences money cannot buy, like pitch-side visits.
- **Matchday personalization.** Through our [customer experience](/services/customer-experience/) work, we designed timely offers and content based on each fan's seat, history and preferences.

## The outcome

In the first full season, monthly active app users grew by 40%, and the app stayed stable through every sold-out game. Loyalty sign-ups doubled, and members now spend 22% more per matchday than they did the season before. Fans have noticed, too: app store ratings rose steadily, and survey feedback points to faster food ordering and *relevant offers* as favorite changes.

The club's commercial team now has a clearer picture of who its fans are and what they value, which helps it plan everything from ticket packages to community events. Internal teams own the app roadmap, and the next phase will bring tailored experiences for fans who follow the team from outside the city.
