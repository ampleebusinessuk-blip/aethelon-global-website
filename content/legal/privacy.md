This notice explains how Aethelon Global collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit this website, get in touch with us, apply to join us, or work with us as a client, partner or supplier. It also explains your rights and how to use them.

We have written it to meet the privacy laws that apply where we operate, including South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act 2013 (POPIA), Morocco’s Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors in those regions, and applicable United States privacy laws.

## The information we collect

### Information you give us

- **When you contact us:** your name, work email, company, job title, phone number (if you give one), the topic you choose and the content of your message.
- **When you apply for a role:** your CV, cover letter, employment and education history, the role you are interested in, your right to work and any other information you choose to include. We do not ask for sensitive information such as health, religion or ethnicity at the application stage. If you choose to share it, we will only use it where the law allows.
- **When you work with us:** business contact details of the people we deal with at clients, partners and suppliers, along with records of our communications, meetings, contracts and invoices.
- **At events or meetings:** business contact details you share with us, for example on a business card.

### Information collected automatically

- **Technical data:** when you visit the site, the server that hosts it records standard information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request, the date and time, and the referring page. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems.
- **Fonts:** our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google’s use of this information is described in its own privacy policy.

This website does **not** use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, social media pixels or cross-site tracking. See our [cookie notice](/cookies/) for details.

### Information from other sources

We may receive business contact details from people who introduce us, from public professional profiles (such as LinkedIn) and from recruitment agencies you have asked to represent you.

## How our contact forms work

Our website forms do not send your information to a server. When you press send, the form opens your own email program with your message ready to send to us. We receive only what you then choose to email. Your email provider will process that message under its own terms.

## How and why we use your information

We only use personal information when we have a lawful reason to do so. Depending on where you are, these are:

- **To reply to your enquiry and talk to you about our services:** our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or the steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract.
- **To assess job applications and run our recruitment process:** taking steps at your request before a possible employment contract, our legitimate interest in hiring the right people, and our legal obligations (such as right-to-work checks).
- **To deliver services to clients and manage our relationships with clients, partners and suppliers:** performing our contracts and our legitimate interest in running our business.
- **To run, secure and improve this website:** our legitimate interest in providing a safe, working website.
- **To send you insights or event invitations:** only where you have asked us to or, where the law allows, where you are an existing business contact. You can opt out at any time.
- **To meet legal and regulatory requirements:** for example tax, accounting and record-keeping, and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.
- **To establish, exercise or defend legal claims.**

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This will not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

We do not use your personal information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only where needed, and only with:

- **Our affiliated companies** in South Africa, the United States and Morocco, so the right team can help you.
- **Service providers** who process information on our behalf, such as website hosting, email and office software, recruitment tools, IT support and professional advisers (accountants, auditors and lawyers). They may only use it to provide their services to us and must keep it secure.
- **Clients**, where you are a candidate we propose for a client project and you have agreed.
- **Authorities, regulators and courts**, where the law requires it or to protect our rights.
- **A buyer or successor**, if all or part of our business is sold or reorganised. They would be required to protect your information as described here.

## International transfers

We work across South Africa, the United States, Morocco and other countries, so your information may be transferred to and stored in countries other than your own. When we do this, we protect it as the law requires, for example by using contracts with recognised data protection clauses, transferring only to organisations bound by equivalent protections, or relying on another lawful ground such as your consent or the performance of a contract with you. Where Moroccan law requires authorisation from or notification to the Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel (CNDP), we will obtain it. You can ask us for more information about the safeguards we use.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the reasons above, then delete or anonymise it:

- **Enquiries and correspondence:** up to two years after our last contact with you, unless they lead to a working relationship.
- **Job applications:** up to twelve months after the recruitment process ends, unless you agree that we may keep your details for future roles. If you join us, your application becomes part of your employee record.
- **Client, partner and supplier records:** for the length of the relationship and up to seven years afterwards, to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.
- **Website server logs:** usually no longer than ninety days, unless needed to investigate a security incident.

## How we protect it

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including access controls, encryption where appropriate, secure suppliers and staff confidentiality obligations. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not email us highly sensitive information unless we ask for it. If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant regulator where the law requires.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Delete** your information, or ask us to stop using it, where we no longer have a valid reason to keep it.
- **Object** to our use of your information, including for direct marketing (which you can always stop).
- **Restrict** how we use your information in certain circumstances.
- **Port** information you gave us to another organisation, where technically possible.
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on it.
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising your privacy rights.

To use any of these rights, email [hello@aethelonglobal.com](mailto:hello@aethelonglobal.com). We may need to confirm your identity before we respond. We will reply within the time the law requires, normally within one month, and we will not charge you unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example:

- **South Africa:** the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za)
- **Morocco:** the Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel, CNDP (cndp.ma)
- **United Kingdom:** the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk)
- **European Union:** the data protection authority in the country where you live or work
- **United States:** the attorney general or consumer protection agency in your state

## Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and professionals. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
