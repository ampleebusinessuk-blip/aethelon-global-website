Manufacturing

We help manufacturers connect planning, operations, and data so they can respond faster and run more efficiently.

Manufacturers are expected to deliver more variety, faster, with less waste, while managing volatile supply chains and a shrinking pool of skilled workers. Too often, sales, operations, and finance plan from different numbers, and factory data stays locked in individual plants. We help manufacturers bring planning together, connect plant and enterprise data, and apply analytics and AI to quality, maintenance, and supply decisions. We also modernize the older systems that hold many operations back, so teams can respond to change with confidence instead of scrambling.

The challenges we hear

Disconnected planning

Sales, operations, and finance often plan from separate spreadsheets and systems, which leads to conflicting numbers, slow decisions, excess inventory, and missed delivery commitments to customers.

Supply chain volatility

Shortages, shipping delays, and sudden shifts in demand make it hard to plan production, and many manufacturers lack the visibility needed to spot problems early and adjust quickly.

Plant data left unused

Machines and production lines generate large volumes of data, but much of it stays in local systems, making it hard to improve quality, predict maintenance needs, or compare performance across plants.

How we help

Unify sales and operations planning

We bring sales, operations, and finance into one planning rhythm with shared data and clear decision rights, so leaders can balance demand, supply, and cost with one trusted plan.

Operating models

Connect plant and enterprise data

We link factory, supply chain, and business data on modern platforms, giving teams the visibility to spot quality issues, track performance across plants, and respond to disruptions faster.

Data & analytics

Modernize core systems

We modernize aging planning and production systems in steady phases, reducing downtime risk and making it easier to adopt new tools for scheduling, quality, and maintenance.
